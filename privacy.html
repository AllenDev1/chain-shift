CHAIN SHIFT

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Chain Shift handles in its web version (allendev1.github.io/chain-shift) and in its iOS and Android apps, and the choices you have.

  • Chain Shift has no accounts, and the game itself never sends your data to us.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The iOS and Android apps show ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • The web version has no ads, no analytics and no cookies.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your level progress, best scores, Daily puzzle results and whether you have seen the tutorial. On the web this is kept in your browser's local storage. In the apps it is kept in the app's own storage. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the site's data in your browser.

Sharing puzzles

When you tap Share, the game creates an image and a link on your device and hands them to your device's share sheet. You choose where they go. We do not receive what you share. Share links contain only a short code describing the puzzle, never personal information.

Advertising in the iOS and Android apps

The apps show full-screen ads between puzzles using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS, if you allow tracking, or the Android advertising ID)
  • your IP address, and a general location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • crash and performance diagnostics for the ads SDK

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads may be personalized based on your interests only where you have allowed it. Otherwise, Google shows non-personalized ads, which are based on general context such as the app and your approximate location.

Your choices

  • Consent: in regions where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. You can change it at any time from Menu → Privacy settings.
  • iOS tracking: the app asks your permission before using your device's advertising identifier. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, under Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on your device.
  • No ads at all: the web version of Chain Shift is free and has no ads.

The web version

The web version has no ads, analytics, trackers or cookies, and makes no requests to third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Chain Shift is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. We do not hold any personal information about you ourselves. For information collected by Google for advertising, you can use your consent choices above and the controls at My Ad Center.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy can be sent to info@thebrilliantideas.com.
