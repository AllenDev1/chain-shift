Common questions

How do I play?

Drag a block and it slides until something stops it. Whatever it hits slides on, setting off a chain reaction. Make the cube stop on the target. Arrows turn anything that slides onto them, pits swallow the first piece in, and walls never move. Open the menu and tap How to play for the full rules, or tap Hint while playing.

I lost my progress.

Progress is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, so it can't be restored after the app is deleted, and it doesn't move between devices. Updating the app keeps your progress.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep Chain Shift free. In the iOS and Android apps, a full-screen ad can appear between puzzles, never during one. The first few puzzles each session are ad-free, and the Daily puzzle and friends' challenges never show ads. The web version at allendev1.github.io/chain-shift has no ads.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where privacy laws apply, open the menu and tap Privacy settings. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I share a puzzle with a friend?

Tap Share after solving a puzzle, or build your own in the Editor and share it from there. Your friend can open the link, or type the puzzle code in Menu → Enter code.

When does the Daily puzzle change?

A new Daily One-Move puzzle arrives every day at midnight, in your device's time zone.
